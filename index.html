FAQ

What is this app?
A quick way to find people and talks at CHI that match your interests. It reads everyone's conference bio and quietly suggests attendees and sessions you might want to check out. No separate account or sign-up needed.
How is the Attendee Interest Map generated?
We turn each person's bio into a set of numbers that captures what their work is about, then compress those numbers down to two dimensions so they can be plotted as dots. People working on similar things end up closer together. It's not a real place, just a visual way of grouping similar interests.

For anyone who wants the technical terms: each bio is turned into an "embedding," a list of numbers produced by a language model that captures the meaning of the text. We then use a technique called UMAP (Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection) to reduce that long list down to just two numbers (an x and a y), while trying to keep similar profiles close together the way they were in the original, higher-dimensional list. Those two numbers are what get plotted as the dot you see on the map.
How are Top Matches generated?
We compare your interest-numbers (see above) to everyone else's and rank people by how similar they are. The closest ones show up as your Top Matches.

Your own co-authors are intentionally left out of this list - you should already be familiar with them, so surfacing them as a "match" isn't useful. Everyone else you haven't co-authored with is still eligible.
How can I actually meet or message someone I find here?
There's no messaging in this app on purpose: it's meant to point you toward people worth meeting in person, not replace that conversation. Think of it as a little scavenger hunt: their affiliation and contribution can tell you where to look, and if they're presenting, their session is a natural place to catch them. You can also ask around: if you know their affiliation, lab, or co-authors, someone there likely knows them and can introduce you. Half the fun of a conference is asking around and making a new friend that way :)

A small circle after someone's name means they have claimed their profile in this app, so they are highly likely at CHI. Everyone else is listed from the conference program and may or may not be here.
How are Top Programs generated?
Same idea, but comparing your interests to each talk or poster's title, to suggest sessions you might not want to miss.
Is there any data collected?
Yes, kept to the minimum needed to run the app: when someone new joins the list, or edits their name, affiliation, or bio, we log that change: the date/time and the associated information. We do not log IP addresses, clicks, searches, or map interactions. Anonymous feedback (the "Share anonymous feedback" button) is logged too: just the message text and when it was sent, with nothing that identifies who sent it.
How do I remove myself from this app?
Everyone on this list is already listed in the official CHI conference program, and that's how your name got here. If you'd like to be removed from this app specifically, contact us below and we'll take care of it.

Where does the conference data come from?
Names, affiliations, paper titles, abstracts and the schedule come from the official CHI programme data (© ACM CHI), licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The starting bio for each person is built from their CHI titles, and the map and matches are computed from the titles and abstracts. Everything derived from that data (the bios, map positions and matches) is shared under the same licence, non-commercially. Paper links go to each paper's DOI.

When does this app go away?
This app is temporary for the conference. It will be shut down at the end of CHI 2027: Friday, May 14, 2027, 11:59 PM Pittsburgh (Eastern) time. After that, it won't be accessible.
